Happy Father's Day

To My Hero & Mentor

Many people see a father as strong — never shaking, always standing. But not everyone sees the behind-the-scenes of those actions. Behind every “I’m fine,” there is always a struggle. A man who wakes up every day, no matter how tired or sick he is, because he has to cater and fend for his family. A man who eats last — and sometimes doesn’t eat at all. A man who sacrifices his own needs for the wants of his family. We celebrate heroes in capes and uniforms, like police officers. But we often fail to celebrate the everyday hero who sacrifices his own life for the sake of others — The one who is the pillar of every family, The one who puts the dreams of his children ahead of his own sleep. So today, I want to celebrate my mentor, my superhero — the one who inspires me to never give up and to keep going. Many might not recognize the behind-the-scenes... Some may not even appreciate the scene itself. But we see you. We love you. And we appreciate you for being our silent warrior.
Happy Father's Day, Dad ❤️
— With love, Samuel Aderogba
